## Authoritative contract

The compact suite follows #158's one-event detail boundary:

- `GET /api/audit-events/<positive-id>/` is protected by the exact centralized
  view policy before lookup or serialization;
- ordinary foreign and nonexistent IDs share the same 404 and fixed safe scope
  log, while staff superusers require an explicit alternate Turnus;
- one complete event is loaded and serialized, never an adjacent event;
- successful delivery first requires one metadata-only `audit.view` detail row
  whose outer resource is `audit_event/<event-id>`;
- detail metadata has exact keys/types/ranges, matching positive event/snapshot
  IDs, fixed result/filter counts, and sensitive count one exactly for a full
  `kid.edit` payload and zero for a legacy payload;
- every access-insertion exception fails closed with sanitized 503 and no event
  payload;
- success, anonymous/authenticated 403, scoped 404, and 503 responses carry the
  exact audit privacy headers.
